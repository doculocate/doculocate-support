Searching by meaning

Write natural-language searches and understand ranked passages and highlights.

Checked against the app · September 27, 2026

On this page

Describe the idea you want to find

Use a short, specific description. Include the subject and the condition that matters. Search queries can contain 1–500 characters.

EXAMPLE SEARCH

situations where the customer can cancel without paying a fee

ILLUSTRATIVE SOURCE PASSAGE
“Either party may discontinue the agreement before expiration without incurring additional charges.”

The search and passage can use different words. Semantic similarity helps retrieve the related idea.

Make a search more specific

  • Instead of termination, try ending the agreement after a delivery delay.
  • Instead of data, try how customer records are handled after account closure.
  • Split a question covering several subjects into separate searches.
  • If a search is too narrow, remove a condition and inspect the broader results.

Read the results

Each result includes the document name, a source excerpt, a relevance label, and available location details. Results are ranked by similarity, with optional AI reranking when configured. The standard app requests up to 10 results; the API supports up to 50.

Relevance is not a confidence guarantee. High, medium, and low labels reflect similarity scores. They are not calibrated probabilities, and they do not verify the correctness or completeness of a document.

Understand highlights

Optional AI reranking can select a quote from a retrieved passage. The application checks the quote against the document before returning a highlight. A result may have no highlight when reranking is disabled, fails, or produces no usable quote. The passage remains available to read.

Inspect the evidence

Use View in document to read surrounding context. A search returning no results does not prove an idea is absent: extraction, wording, and retrieval limits can affect what is found. Search supports your review; it does not replace professional judgment.
